ACTIVITE - WRITING SENTENCES ABOUT YOUR DAILY ROUTINE

My routine starts on Sunday night, when I check my clothes, my materials 
for class on Monday, and my agenda. In the past, I was careful about my 
routine, but today I don't pay as much attention to it.
After that, on Monday, I have classes at college in the morning, and then 
I relax in the afternoon. In the evening, I go to my English class.
On Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday, I just go to college. After that, I 
relax, focus on my programming projects, and spend time with my family.
On Friday, I go to college, as always, and in the afternoon, I go back to 
college to take a subject that I missed last semester. I didn't forget it. 
I just couldn't take it because I had training at the same time. Since I 
couldn't miss another subject this semester, I decided to take this subject 
in the afternoon.
On Friday night, I rarely stay at home. I go to church, my girlfriend's 
house, my grandparents' house, or I just stay with my friends.
On weekends, I normally work on Saturday, and at night, I go to church. On 
Sunday morning, I always go to church because I'm part of the dance group. 
We are the best!
And finally, I go back to the beginning of my routine. :)
